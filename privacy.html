Privacy Policy

What vanilla processes and why.

YOUR PRIVACY

1. Overview

This policy explains how the vanilla Discord bot and its website process information when people use bot commands or site features. The bot does not require a separate vanilla account. Discord and any third-party service you choose to use have their own privacy practices.

INFORMATION PROCESSED

2. Data the bot uses

Depending on which features a server enables and which commands a person uses, the bot may process:

  • Discord user, server, channel, and role IDs, plus names or tags needed to show command results.
  • Server settings, command configuration, role and channel settings, and moderation records such as actions, reasons, and the people involved.
  • Message or event details needed to run commands, moderation, automod, AI, or administrator-configured logs. If logging is enabled, configured log channels may receive deleted or edited message details and member or voice events.
  • Feature records such as AFK messages, reminders, leveling and activity counts, profiles and social links, playlists, favorites, and connected Last.fm account names.

The exact information depends on the command and configuration used. Server administrators choose whether logging and some moderation features are enabled.

PURPOSE

3. How data is used

The bot uses information to run commands, remember settings and feature state, protect servers, keep configured logs, and troubleshoot failures. It stores feature records in the bot operator’s configured data store so those features continue to work across sessions.

If you use an AI feature, the prompt and relevant recent conversation context may be sent to Groq to generate a response. AI conversation history is stored with Discord user, server, and channel IDs and is automatically cleaned up after 30 days by the bot.

SERVICE PROVIDERS

4. Sharing

Information is shared with Discord as needed to deliver bot commands and responses. When you invoke a feature that depends on another provider, the relevant prompt, search query, URL, or account handle may be sent to that provider. This includes Groq for AI requests and music, Last.fm, search, or media providers for their related features.

Server logs may be visible to people who can access the log channels chosen by that server’s administrators. The vanilla website does not sell personal information.

STORAGE

5. Retention

AI conversation history is automatically deleted after 30 days. Other settings, logs, and feature records are kept while needed to provide the feature or until they are removed by the bot, the server, or the maintainers. Retention can vary by feature and server configuration.

Removing the bot stops future processing for that server but may not immediately remove records already stored by the bot, posted into Discord channels, or held by third-party providers.

WEBSITE

6. This website

The command page saves the text prefix you enter in your browser’s local storage so copied commands can use it. This setting stays in that browser until you clear it. The site does not ask you to log in and currently does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts when internet access is available; that request may expose standard connection information such as your IP address to Google. System fonts are used if the font service is unavailable.

REQUESTS

7. Your choices

You can clear the saved prefix using your browser’s site storage settings. For bot data access or deletion requests, contact the maintainers through the support server and include the relevant Discord user or server ID and the feature involved. A server owner or administrator may need to identify records belonging to their server.

Server administrators can disable optional logging or remove the bot. Requests cannot erase information already retained by Discord or an external provider under that provider’s own policies.

UPDATES

8. Updates and contact

This policy may change when bot features or data handling change. The current version and its update date will appear here. For privacy questions or requests, contact the vanilla maintainers through the support server.

The bot operator’s legal name, business address, and jurisdiction are not listed in the source provided for this site. Add the responsible operator’s contact details before treating this as a final legal notice.
